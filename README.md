# InterviewIQ.AI 🎯

InterviewIQ.AI is an AI-powered mock interview platform designed to help students and developers practice interviews in a more realistic way.

You can choose an interview type, provide your profile details or resume, answer AI-generated questions, and get feedback on your performance.

## ✨ Features

- 🔐 Google Authentication
- 🎯 Technical & HR Interviews
- 📄 Resume Upload & Analysis
- 🤖 AI-generated Interview Questions
- 🎤 Voice-based Interview Experience
- ⏱️ Timed Questions
- 🧠 AI-powered Answer Evaluation
- 📊 Performance Analysis
- 📈 Interview History
- 📝 Detailed Feedback
- 📥 Download Interview Report as PDF
- 💳 Credit-based Interview System
- 💰 Razorpay Payment Integration
- 📱 Responsive Interface

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Sign in using Google.
2. Select Technical or HR interview.
3. Enter your role, experience and other details.
4. Upload your resume if you want a personalized interview.
5. AI generates interview questions based on your profile.
6. Answer the questions using text or voice.
7. AI evaluates your answers.
8. Get scores and feedback for your performance.
9. View your complete interview report.
10. Download the report as a PDF.
